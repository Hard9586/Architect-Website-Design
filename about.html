Our Story

How It All Began

Our story began in 2014, when founder Harsh Mehta — fresh from studies at CEPT University Ahmedabad and a transformative stint with Zaha Hadid Architects in London — returned home with a singular, bold vision: to build an architecture practice that would redefine the built environment of India's fastest-growing cities.

What started as a two-person studio tucked in a small Ahmedabad office has since evolved into a 40-member powerhouse recognized across India for innovation, sustainability, and client-centric design. Our portfolio spans residential masterpieces, city-defining commercial towers, hospitality landmarks, and far-sighted urban planning projects.

Today, HM Architect stands at the intersection of art and engineering — a studio where every project is approached not as a construction exercise but as an opportunity to write a new chapter in the story of the spaces we inhabit. We believe great architecture must serve people, honor context, and stand the test of time.

Our multidisciplinary team of architects, urban designers, interior specialists, and sustainability consultants brings together deep expertise and creative energy to deliver work that goes beyond expectations — every single time.

Purpose & Direction

Mission & Vision

The guiding principles that inform every decision we make — from the first sketch to the final key handed over

Our Mission

To design built environments that enhance human well-being, celebrate local context, and create lasting value for the communities and clients we serve — while continuously advancing the art and science of architecture. We exist to turn your most ambitious vision into a reality that surpasses it.

Every project, regardless of scale or budget, receives our full creative and technical attention. We do not compromise. We do not cut corners. We deliver excellence.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as India's most innovative and trusted architecture studio — setting new, enduring standards for design excellence, environmental responsibility, and client-centric practice that inspire the next generation of architects and builders.

We envision a built world where beauty and function coexist effortlessly, where every structure tells a story, and where communities thrive within thoughtfully designed spaces that stand for generations.

How We Work

Our Design Process

A methodical, collaborative, and transparent 8-step journey from your first brief to your final key handover

01

Discovery & Brief

Deep listening sessions to understand your vision, lifestyle, aspirations, budget, and timeline constraints before we sketch a single line.

02

Site Analysis

Thorough study of topography, climate, orientation, neighbourhood context, and regulatory constraints informing the design opportunity.

03

Concept Design

Translation of your brief and site analysis into initial design alternatives — spatial narratives, mood boards, and rough massing models.

04

Design Development

Refinement of the chosen concept into detailed drawings, 3D visualizations, material selections, and interior design integration.

05

Construction Documents

Comprehensive technical drawing sets, specifications, and Bill of Quantities prepared for tender and statutory approvals.

06

Approvals & Permitting

Full support through the municipal and statutory approval process — liaising with authorities, consultants, and structural engineers.

07

Construction Supervision

Regular site visits, quality checks, contractor coordination, and design clarifications ensure the built reality matches the design intent.

08

Handover & Aftercare

Final snagging, documentation handover, and 12-month post-occupancy aftercare — because our relationship doesn't end at completion.
