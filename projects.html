Project Spotlight

Featured Project

Award Winner · 2015

Skyline Villa, Ahmedabad

Our first residential landmark remains among our most celebrated. Skyline Villa is a sculptural 6,500 sq ft home perched on Ahmedabad's western ridge, designed to frame the Arabian Sea horizon at every turn. The design synthesizes exposed board-formed concrete, locally quarried Kota limestone, and hand-selected teak timber into a composition that feels simultaneously monumental and intimately human.

The project won the Gujarat Regional Design Award and has been featured in Architectural Digest India, Vogue Living, and six international design publications. It remains a pilgrimage site for architecture students from across the country.
