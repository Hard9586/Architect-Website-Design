Architectural Design

Architecture is the art of shaping space to serve life. At HM Architect, we approach every design challenge as an opportunity to create something that raises the human experience — buildings that are not just beautiful, but deeply meaningful, climate-responsive, and technically rigorous.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest, straightforward answers to the questions we hear most often from prospective clients

How long does a typical architectural design project take?
Timeline varies significantly by project type and scope. A luxury residence typically takes 6–8 months for design and documentation, followed by 12–18 months of construction. A mid-size commercial project may require 10–14 months of design and 18–30 months to build. We provide a detailed, realistic programme at the outset of every project and manage to it rigorously.
What is your fee structure and how is it calculated?
Our fees are typically calculated as a percentage of the estimated construction cost, ranging from 4–12% depending on project complexity, typology, and the scope of services engaged. For some project types we offer fixed-fee or hourly arrangements. We always discuss and agree fees transparently before commencing any work. Contact us for a tailored fee proposal.
Do you work on projects outside Ahmedabad and Gujarat?
Absolutely. HM Architect currently has active projects across Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Goa, Delhi NCR, and Rajasthan. We also have an active international project in Dubai through our partnership with ZA Studio. We are fully equipped to manage remote projects through video consultations, site visits, and on-ground project management coordinators.
Can you help with building permits, approvals, and regulatory submissions?
Yes. Navigating the approval process is an integral part of our comprehensive design service. Our team prepares all necessary drawings and documentation for statutory submissions to municipal corporations, development authorities, and environmental clearance bodies. We maintain strong working relationships with local authorities in all the cities where we regularly practise.
Do you provide 3D visualizations for all projects?
Photorealistic 3D visualizations are included in our Professional and Premium service packages and can be added to the Essential package. We believe strongly that clients deserve to see — and fall in love with — their project before construction begins. Our renders are of the highest commercial quality and have been used for award submissions, marketing campaigns, and client presentations.
What is the minimum project size or budget you accept?
We do not publish a minimum project size, as our engagement depends more on project complexity and alignment with our studio's strengths than on size alone. We have worked on intimate residential interventions of ₹1 crore and large mixed-use developments exceeding ₹500 crore. Contact us with your brief and we will let you know honestly whether we are the right fit.
How does HM Architect approach sustainable and green design?
Sustainability is not an add-on at HM Architect — it is embedded in our design DNA. Every project is oriented and massed for optimum solar and wind performance, with passive design strategies explored before any mechanical system is specified. Our sustainability team includes LEED APs and GRIHA evaluators. We have helped clients achieve IGBC Green Building certifications across multiple project typologies.
Do you also manage the construction process or only the design?
We offer both. Most clients engage us for full design services including construction-phase supervision, where our architects make regular site visits to verify quality, resolve on-site design queries, and certify contractor claims. For clients who want even more hands-on management, our Project Management service provides a dedicated PM who is essentially your representative on site full-time throughout the build.
How many projects does HM Architect work on simultaneously?
Our studio actively manages between 12–18 projects at any given time across design and construction stages. Our structured team model ensures that every project has a dedicated design architect, project architect, and project manager reporting to a director — so your project always receives the focused attention it deserves, regardless of how many others are in the studio.
What happens if I change my mind mid-design?
Design is an iterative, collaborative process and we fully expect refinements along the way. Our design stages are structured to capture and incorporate your feedback at each gateway before proceeding. Significant changes of scope after a stage has been completed may incur an additional fee, which we always discuss openly before proceeding — there are never any surprises in our billing.
